instructions:

in this GAME:

1.when 3 gems destryed we get 3 points
2.when 4 gems destryed we get 4 points and makes fire gem
3.when 5 gems destryed we get 5 points and makes xgem
4.fire gem destroy 9 gems in a block and we get 10 points
5.xgem destroy all gems in the same row and column and we get 100 points
6.game will end after 1 minute
7.use arrow keys to move the cursor 
8.press enter to select and deselect the gem
9.use arrow keys to swap
